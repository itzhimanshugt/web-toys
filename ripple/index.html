Readouts

Stable step. The explicit five-point scheme is stable when the Courant number v·Δt/Δx stays at or below 1/√2 ≈ 0.707. This tank runs at 0.50 in deep water and 0.30 over the shallows.

Why shallows bend waves. Long water waves travel at about √(g·h), so a thinner layer is slower. A glass plate under a real ripple tank acts as a lens the same way glass slows light. Here, 150 mm/s against 250 mm/s gives a refractive index of 5/3.

Bow waves. A source faster than its waves trails a cone with half-angle μ where sin μ = 1/M. Set drift above 1.0 in preset 7 to see the predicted cone drawn over the simulated one.

Model limits. Real water ripples are dispersive: below about 17 mm wavelength surface tension takes over from gravity. This tank uses the ideal non-dispersive equation so v stays fixed and λ = v / f holds exactly, up to small grid dispersion.
