In Galactic Bridges and Tails (1972), Alar and Juri Toomre used this restricted three-body method to show that tails and bridges are tidal. Prograde passes, where a disk spins the same way as the orbit, throw the longest tails. A companion dropped through a disk along its axis drives an expanding ring wave (Lynds & Toomre, 1976).

Units: G = 1, mass of A = 1, radius of disk A = 1. Tilt is measured from the orbital plane; turn rotates the tilt axis. Pericentre 0 is a head-on fall along the line of centres. Keys: Space, R, L, V, 1 to 4, [ and ].
