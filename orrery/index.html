    Kepler's third law: a planet's period squared is proportional to its orbit radius cubed. Here 1 AU orbits in one 4-beat bar, so T = 4 · a1.5 beats. Kepler stated it in Harmonices Mundi (1619), the same book in which he gave each planet a melody.
