Field notes

  • Gravity assist. Pass behind a moving moon and you borrow some of its orbital momentum. Voyager 2 chained Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus to reach Neptune in 1989.
  • Capture radius. Around a non-rotating black hole no circular orbit is stable inside three Schwarzschild radii. The real point of no return, the event horizon, sits at one. Here the dashed red ring is the capture radius: cross it and the probe is lost.
  • Integrator. Flights step at a fixed 1/120 s with kick-drift-kick leapfrog, a symplectic method: orbital energy wobbles but does not drift away.
