How it works

The yellow is trail laid down by 61,277 particles. Each step, a particle smells the trail at three points ahead, turns toward the strongest, moves one cell and drops a little more trail. The trail spreads and fades a bit every step.

That is the whole rule. The veins, loops and constant rewiring come from many particles following it at once. This is Jeff Jones's 2010 agent model of Physarum.

The real organism does this with oats too. In 2010, Atsushi Tero and colleagues placed oat flakes where the cities around Tokyo sit, and the slime mold grew a network comparable to the region's rail system in efficiency, cost and fault tolerance.
