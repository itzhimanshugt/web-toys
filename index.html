Web Toys

8 single-file interactive pieces. No build step, no dependencies.

  • Athanor Sand BenchA falling-sand chemistry sandbox where 21 materials fall, flow, burn, melt, dissolve and explode across a live temperature field.
  • Brass Orrery SynthA clockwork orrery you play as an instrument: launch planets whose Kepler orbits strike notes each time they cross the meridian.
  • Bulb DiveA real-time raymarched explorer for the Mandelbulb, Mandelbox, Menger sponge and quaternion Julia fractals, with orbit, deep zoom, fly-to and autopilot.
  • Periapsis RunA gravity slingshot puzzle: pull back to launch a probe past planets, moving moons, wormholes and a black hole to reach the beacon.
  • Phase Space SculptureHundreds of thousands of particles trace eight classic strange attractors in 3D, with live parameters, trails and morphing between systems.
  • Physarum Petri DishA slime mold simulation that grows networks between oat flakes you drop on the plate.
  • Rain CarverRain carves river valleys into a procedurally generated mountain block, one simulated droplet at a time, in live 3D.
  • Turing GardenA Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion garden running on the GPU: paint chemical V, pick feed and kill rates on a live parameter map and watch spots, worms, mazes and coral grow.
